noise is designed to work without your phone number, email address, legal name, contact list, or location. This policy explains the information our central service still needs to deliver, synchronize, secure, and moderate noise.

01

Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the noise desktop applications, web application, mobile applications, marketing website, and central service operated by Gnosys Labs (together, the “Gnosys Labs Services”). “noise,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Gnosys Labs.

The official noise apps use the Gnosys Labs central service rather than user-selected infrastructure. Third-party infrastructure and media-search providers remain separate services with their own privacy practices.

02

Information noise processes

Account and profile information

noise creates a random noise ID and cryptographic identity. Your chosen display name, avatar, and bio are profile information. noise does not require a phone number, email address, legal name, address book, or location to create an account. The app asks for a birth date to confirm that you are at least 18, but performs that check on your device and stores only an age-attested setting—not the birth date itself.

Your password is used on your device to derive the credentials that unlock your encrypted account vault. Gnosys Labs does not receive or store your plaintext password and cannot recover a lost noise ID or password for you.

Messages, group activity, and media

noise clients create signed group, direct-message, membership, moderation, and profile events. Message bodies and media are encrypted on your device before they are sent. The central service stores encrypted events and account vaults, together with public keys, signatures, group and thread relationships, object identifiers, timestamps, delivery and read state, safety state, deletion records, and other protocol data needed to authenticate, deliver, synchronize, moderate, and delete content.

Encrypted media is stored as complete encrypted objects in private Cloudflare R2 object storage. Gnosys Labs and Cloudflare receive the encrypted object and related storage metadata, but not the key held by noise clients to decrypt it.

Safety reports

If you choose “report to noise” for a serious safety issue, your app sends an end-to-end encrypted report to the noise safety service. A report may include the reported message text, your selected reason and description, group and event identifiers, signed profile snapshots, public keys, timestamps, signatures, encrypted-object identifiers, and locally computed hashes. It does not include reported media bytes or media decryption keys. Authorized reviewers can decrypt reports and may issue signed restrictions enforced by official noise apps.

Device and browser storage

Desktop apps keep local identity state, message state, preferences, and media caches on your device. The web app stores an encrypted browser vault in IndexedDB and protects it with a non-exportable key generated by your browser. Clearing site data, using private browsing, browser resets, operating-system backups, or device-management tools can affect or copy that local data.

Network and diagnostic information

When your device connects, network providers necessarily process information such as IP addresses, request timing, user-agent information, and traffic volume. The noise central service and its hosting and security providers may receive this network-level information. Encrypted media is fetched through the service’s object-storage path; centralization does not make network metadata end-to-end encrypted.

The Gnosys Labs website and service are not designed for advertising or behavioral tracking. Our hosting, security, database, and network providers may process request and security data to deliver and protect the service. The marketing site contacts GitHub’s API to locate the current noise release when a download link is shown.

GIF, sticker, clip, and link services

If you use the GIF keyboard, the central service sends your search terms and request settings to KLIPY and returns media results to your app. Loading or sending a selected result may also contact the media host identified in that result. If you request a link preview, the central service fetches the linked webpage to create that preview. Those providers may process request and network-level information under their own policies.

Communications with us

If you email us or report a problem, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and the contents of your message.

03

How we use information

We process information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • create, restore, synchronize, and secure noise identities;
  • route and make encrypted groups, messages, profiles, and media available;
  • deliver device notifications and provide requested GIF and link services;
  • provide moderation, deletion, abuse prevention, and network-integrity features;
  • diagnose failures, protect the service, and improve reliability;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests; and
  • comply with law and enforce the Terms of Service.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use noise content for targeted advertising, build advertising profiles, or operate a public people or group directory.

04

When information is shared

Information may be processed or disclosed to:

  • Infrastructure providers. Hosting, content-delivery, DDoS protection, database, object-storage, and similar providers—including Cloudflare for encrypted media storage—process the data needed to provide their services.
  • Feature providers. Apple processes iOS notification routing information, KLIPY processes GIF-keyboard requests, and GitHub processes release lookups from the marketing site. A linked webpage receives a request from our service when you ask noise to create a preview.
  • Other noise users. People in a shared group or direct-message thread receive the content and profile information you choose to share.
  • Legal and safety recipients. We may preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or defend the service.
  • A successor. If the service or organization is reorganized, acquired, or transferred, information may transfer with it subject to applicable law.

Encryption limits what Gnosys Labs and infrastructure providers can read, but it does not stop a recipient from saving, exporting, forwarding, or capturing content after their client decrypts it.

05

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until the app removes it, you log out, leave a group, delete your account, clear browser data, uninstall the app, or remove it through your operating system. Operating-system and device backups may retain copies beyond that point.

Central-service data remains available for the time needed to operate the service, maintain account and group state, prevent abuse, meet legal duties, or until it is deleted or expires under the applicable feature. Session challenges, sessions, disappearing-message state, and temporary safety restrictions have feature-specific expiry times. Encrypted message events and media may remain until a valid deletion is processed, an account or group is deleted, or the service removes them under its policies.

Deletion still has real limits.

noise cannot guarantee erasure from screenshots, exports, backups, modified clients, recipients’ devices, or offline devices that have not received a deletion instruction.

Support and legal correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, protect the service, and meet legal obligations.

06

Security and early-alpha limits

noise uses cryptographic identities, signed events, on-device encryption, encrypted account vaults, encrypted push tokens, and private encrypted media storage. No system is perfectly secure. noise is early-alpha software, and its protocol and implementation have not been represented as a completed independent security audit. Do not rely on noise as a life-safety system or a guarantee of high-risk anonymity.

07

International processing

noise is available over the internet. Gnosys Labs, infrastructure and feature providers, and other users may process information in countries other than your own. Privacy protections and government-access rules vary by location.

08

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to appeal or complain to a privacy regulator. You may also be entitled to know the categories and sources of personal information we process and the recipients to whom it is disclosed.

You can change profile information and use deletion controls in the app. You may also email admin@gnosyslabs.xyz. Because noise does not require an email address or legal identity, we may need your noise ID or cryptographic proof from your account to locate data and verify that a request is yours. We cannot decrypt content or recover credentials we do not have.

09

Children

noise is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old to use the Gnosys Labs Services. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service we operate, contact us so we can investigate.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as noise changes. We will post the revised policy here and change the “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be announced in the app, on the website, or through the project repository.

11

Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or reports about this policy can be sent to admin@gnosyslabs.xyz.
