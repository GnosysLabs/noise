01

Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the noise web application, marketing website, and central service operated by Gnosys Labs (together, the “Gnosys Labs Services”). “noise,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Gnosys Labs.

The noise web app uses the Gnosys Labs central service rather than user-selected infrastructure. Third-party infrastructure and linked services have their own privacy practices.

02

Information noise processes

Account and profile information

Creating an account requires an email address, a unique username, a display name, and a password. Your profile may also include an avatar, banner, bio, status, and other information you choose to share. Account records, profile information, friends, blocks, space memberships, and preferences are stored by the service. A phone number, address book, or location is not required to register.

Your password is sent over HTTPS for account authentication. The service stores a salted password hash rather than the plaintext password. Signed-in sessions use a secure browser cookie. If you enable an authenticator, the service stores an encrypted authenticator secret and hashed recovery codes, together with session and security records needed to manage account access.

Messages, group activity, and media

The central service stores message text, uploaded files, profiles, space and channel information, memberships, reactions, timestamps, and moderation records. It also stores read state, notifications, drafts, saved messages, and reminders so those features can work across signed-in browsers. Access is controlled by account, membership, and channel permissions.

Connections to the official service use HTTPS to encrypt data in transit. Messages and uploads are not end-to-end encrypted: the service processes and stores their contents, and server operators can access them. Do not treat noise as a service in which only recipients hold the keys to message content.

Safety and moderation

The service processes blocks, message requests, role and channel permissions, bans, timeouts, and moderation actions. These records help enforce access and safety controls. If you contact us about abuse or a safety issue, we receive the information and content you choose to provide.

Device and browser storage

The web app uses cookies for signed-in sessions and browser storage for items such as conversation drafts, interface preferences, selected conversations, and media-device choices. This local storage is not an encrypted account vault. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove local state; it does not delete content already stored by the service.

Network and diagnostic information

When your device connects, network providers necessarily process information such as IP addresses, request timing, user-agent information, and traffic volume. The noise central service and its hosting and security providers may receive this network-level information. HTTPS protects data in transit but does not hide connection metadata from the providers that handle the connection.

The Gnosys Labs website and service are not designed for advertising or behavioral tracking. Our hosting, security, database, and network providers may process request and security data to deliver and protect the service. The data they process is subject to their own policies.

Calls, shared media, and links

When you join a voice or video call, your browser requests access to the microphone, camera, or screen you choose to share. The service processes call membership and connection information, and media infrastructure carries the audio, video, or screen content you transmit. Other call participants receive the content you share. Opening a link or externally hosted media may contact that website or host, which can process request information under its own policy.

Communications with us

If you email us or report a problem, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and the contents of your message.

03

How we use information

We process information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • create, synchronize, and secure noise accounts;
  • deliver spaces, channels, messages, profiles, uploads, and calls;
  • provide in-app notifications, saved messages, reminders, and draft synchronization;
  • provide moderation, deletion, abuse prevention, and network-integrity features;
  • diagnose failures, protect the service, and improve reliability;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests; and
  • comply with law and enforce the Terms of Service.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use noise content for targeted advertising or build advertising profiles.

04

When information is shared

Information may be processed or disclosed to:

  • Infrastructure providers. Hosting, content-delivery, DDoS protection, database, storage, media relay, and similar providers process the data needed to provide their services.
  • Linked services. Websites and external media hosts you open receive requests associated with those actions and apply their own privacy policies.
  • Other noise users. People with access to a shared space, channel, conversation, or call receive the content and profile information you share there. Anyone with a valid space invite can view its preview, including its name, icon, banner, description, and aggregate member counts, before joining; the preview does not include private messages or a member list.
  • Legal and safety recipients. We may preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or defend the service.
  • A successor. If the service or organization is reorganized, acquired, or transferred, information may transfer with it subject to applicable law.

Access controls do not stop a recipient from saving, exporting, forwarding, or capturing content they can view. Messages and uploads are not end-to-end encrypted.

05

Retention and deletion

Local browser data can remain until the app removes it or you clear site data. Logging out ends the session but does not necessarily clear every local draft, preference, cached file, or backup. Clearing local data does not delete the corresponding records stored by the service.

Central-service data remains available for the time needed to operate the service, maintain account and group state, prevent abuse, meet legal duties, or until it is deleted or expires under the applicable feature. Sessions, sign-in challenges, invites, and timeouts have feature-specific expiry times. Messages, uploads, account records, and other stored data may remain until a supported deletion is processed or the service removes them under its policies.

Deletion still has real limits.

noise cannot guarantee erasure from screenshots, exports, backups, modified clients, recipients’ devices, or offline devices that have not received a deletion instruction.

Support and legal correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, protect the service, and meet legal obligations.

06

Security and early-alpha limits

noise uses HTTPS, salted password hashes, secure session cookies, and account and channel access controls. Optional authenticator-based sign-in and session management provide additional account controls. These protections are not end-to-end encryption. No system is perfectly secure. noise is early-alpha software, and its implementation has not been represented as a completed independent security audit. Do not rely on noise as a life-safety system or a guarantee of high-risk anonymity.

07

International processing

noise is available over the internet. Gnosys Labs, infrastructure and feature providers, and other users may process information in countries other than your own. Privacy protections and government-access rules vary by location.

08

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to appeal or complain to a privacy regulator. You may also be entitled to know the categories and sources of personal information we process and the recipients to whom it is disclosed.

You can change profile information and use deletion controls in the app. You may also email admin@gnosyslabs.xyz. Because account requests must be verified, we may need your username, account email, or other appropriate information to locate records and confirm that the request is yours.

09

Children

noise is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old to use the Gnosys Labs Services. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service we operate, contact us so we can investigate.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as noise changes. We will post the revised policy here and change the “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be announced in the app, on the website, or through the project repository.

11
