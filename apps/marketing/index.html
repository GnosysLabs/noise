WINDOWS ALPHA / ONE THING FIRST

WINDOWS MIGHT TRY TO SCARE YOU.

noise is new, so SmartScreen may call it an unrecognized app. That means the installer is still building download reputation. It does not mean that Windows found malware.

01
OPEN THE INSTALLER.

Stay on the “Windows protected your PC” screen.

02
CLICK “MORE INFO.”

Windows will show the filename and another option.

03
CLICK “RUN ANYWAY.”

The normal noise setup will begin.

WHY THIS HAPPENS

SmartScreen leans on download history and signing reputation. noise is open source, and every release comes from the public repository.
