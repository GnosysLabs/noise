A FEW THINGS BEFORE YOU COME IN

GOOD QUESTIONS.

Do I need to install anything?

You can use noise in your browser at app.makenoise.chat, but we recommend the desktop app for the best experience. Download noise on itch.io, create an account, then make a server or join one with an invite.

What’s a server?

A home for a group of people, with its own text and voice channels, members, roles, icon, and banner. Keep game night, band practice, and the bigger community organized in their own corners.

Can I just message a friend?

Yes. Add friends and start a direct message, or bring several friends into a group DM. You don’t need to create a server for every conversation.

Are messages end-to-end encrypted?

Not currently. Connections are encrypted in transit, but the service stores messages and uploads to provide chat and history. Calls use encrypted transport too; noise does not currently offer end-to-end encryption. Read how your data is handled.

Is noise finished?

noise is in early alpha. The core servers, messaging, and calling features are here, and we’re still improving them. Bring your friends and tell us what needs work.
